New patients welcome

Your first visit

Your first appointment takes about an hour. We take digital X-rays, check your gums and teeth, talk through anything that worries you and give you a clear plan with prices before any treatment starts. New patients receive a complimentary whitening kit after their first cleaning.

Insurance and financing

We work with most major PPO insurance plans and file the claims for you. No insurance? Our Nova Smile membership covers two cleanings, exams and X-rays a year for one monthly fee, and interest-free financing is available for larger treatments such as implants and aligners.
