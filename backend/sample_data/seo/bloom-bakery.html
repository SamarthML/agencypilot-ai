Baked this morning, loved all day

Bloom Bakery is a family-owned bakery with three neighbourhood shops in Austin: East Austin, Mueller and South Lamar. Our bakers start at 4am so the sourdough is still crackly and the cardamom morning buns are still warm when we open the doors at 7.

Order for pickup or delivery

Neighbourhood favourites

Country sourdough

A 36-hour ferment with flour from Texas farms. Order a whole loaf or a half for the week.

Cardamom morning buns

Laminated dough, orange zest and cardamom sugar. Our most loved pastry since 2017, best with a cortado on a Saturday morning.

Seasonal fruit galettes

Whatever the farmers market has this week, folded into a buttery crust. This autumn it is pear and ginger, then cranberry and orange for the holidays.

Celebration cakes, made to order

Birthdays, graduations and holiday tables deserve a cake that tastes as good as it looks. Choose from chocolate olive oil, vanilla bean with strawberry, or carrot cake with brown butter frosting. Order one to two weeks ahead; we write the message by hand.

Pastry boxes for the office

Tuesday to Thursday we deliver pastry boxes across central Austin before 9am, with easy invoicing for office managers. Mix morning buns, scones and savoury croissants for teams of ten to fifty.

Thanksgiving pre-orders are open

Reserve pies, rolls and holiday cookie boxes now and pick them up from your nearest shop on Wednesday or Thursday morning. Pre-orders close on November 22 or when we sell out.
